# Connected carousel product flow

In Products, choose **Several products - swipe cards**. The source picker offers saved products, physical Product research, Discovery ideas, and active Saved competitor ads. It loads the workspace's complete saved collection rather than only the compact overview page. Search and pagination keep the list manageable; selected products remain selected across source/page changes. The carousel mode, selection, collection name/URL and budget are saved in this tab for the current user and workspace.

An existing linked product is selected or edited rather than duplicated. A new source opens the normal actual-product form over the carousel. Saving selects the resulting product and preserves the builder inputs. Cancelling preserves the unfinished product draft. Research/Discovery can prefill the idea's name/category and, for a research brief, description for owner confirmation. Competitor ads prefill only evidence selection and an origin reference: competitor copy, price, image and URL are never turned into owner product facts. Uploaded product images, actual selling price, costs, stock and own destination must be supplied or confirmed by the owner.

The source reference is validated on the server for workspace ownership and physical-product suitability. Archived ads and missing discovery candidates cannot be newly attached. An origin pointer can be detached without deleting the independent product. Deleting a source detaches surviving product pointers. Digital service research is excluded from the physical-product picker.

Group research receives the saved research summaries, original decision/version/status, findings, competitor leads and risks as dated context. It also receives the selected products' linked Ad Library observations and actual product images through the existing research pipeline. Earlier AI hypotheses or human approval are not proof of demand, current evidence, or approval for a new campaign. The carousel still gets a fresh combined research/plan and its own review; separate product economics and one shared capped budget remain in force.

New group plans snapshot a fingerprint of their saved research context. Research changes supersede affected pending/approved launch reviews and mark unlaunched plans as needing research; approved historical snapshots remain intact. Submission/execution and plan reuse also check the fingerprint. Recorded manual campaigns and already created/active campaigns are not changed by this invalidation. Missing product/card facts block preparation in the picker and remain validated on the server.

Coverage: backend tests for provenance, workspace isolation, source reuse, report reuse, changed-source approval invalidation, history preservation and source deletion; browser tests for the full source-to-product-to-carousel flow, saved selection after reload, no duplicate product, safe competitor drafts, incomplete-product correction, and Bangla mobile layout. Existing carousel/manual approval, catalogue, Ad Library handoff and physical-product research regression tests remain applicable.
