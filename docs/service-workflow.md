# Services and Service research

**Services** holds actual digital service/software profiles. An owner saves the offer, buyer, deliverables, differentiation, portfolio notes, target country, optional regions, dated evidence and competitor ads. Optional price/cost/close-rate/creative preferences seed campaign preparation. Unknown economics remain null; profile price currency is explicit and never silently converted to the ad account currency.

Opening a saved service creates or reuses its single-country workspace without running an LLM. The same profile/country retains its project and version history. A changed country gets a separate project; earlier work is retained. The research model already configured for the workspace handles the existing high-thinking market research task. The single-country prompt and required assessment schema produce test/revise/hold, offer positioning, buyer/decision process, acquisition approach, offer tests, validation steps, stopping criteria and evidence gaps. Uploaded image creatives use the existing private-image analysis/cache path; proof notes remain owner assertions. Existing Ad Library analysis, dated sources, supported search retrieval and recorded results feed the report. No demand, competitor performance, CAC or sales forecast is invented to fill gaps.

An unchanged analysis is reused by the existing 12-hour evidence cache. Source/profile changes or an explicit fresh check require new analysis. Independent evidence and measured business outcomes retain their original confidence/provenance rules; a selected country or a stronger model is not proof of market demand. Hold clears the recommended test country. A human can record an explicit bounded-test decision in a new reviewed version; campaign preparation requires the test verdict and approved latest research. The campaign draft still has a separate approval and manual/direct launch path.

**Service research** is the renamed Research Studio for comparing candidate countries. It can start from a saved service, preserving the existing free-form comparison draft separately. It supports previous briefs, regional comparison, follow-up research and approval. Single-country saved-service workspaces do not clutter the comparison list. Old saved `Research studio` navigation values continue to open the renamed page.

Profiles and projects are workspace-scoped. Profile edits invalidate affected research and unlaunched campaign approvals while retaining original approved snapshots. The research save, review and campaign checks bind to the profile facts. A concurrent change cannot approve stale results. Already recorded/manual/launched campaigns are not altered. Country workspaces are serialized and have a unique partial database index. Independent profile deletion uses the existing preview/confirmation/cascade safeguards, including active-campaign protection. Media/evidence deletion detaches profile references and invalidates dependent drafts. All service records and indexes are included in encrypted backups.

Validation covers profile persistence, single-country and comparison separation, cache reuse, strict model assessment, hold/test review, facts/creative/economics reuse, changed-profile invalidation, source races, tenant boundaries, deletion and Bangla mobile layout. Demo results are explicitly simulated; read-only production verification does not run research or activate ads.
